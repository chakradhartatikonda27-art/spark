Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting SPARK Beauty Salon.

Do I need an appointment, or are walk-ins welcome?

We welcome walk-ins based on real-time station availability. However, we strongly recommend reserving your slot online or via WhatsApp to guarantee zero waiting time with your preferred specialist.

What haircare and color formulation brands do you use?

We exclusively use genuine professional lines, principally L'Oréal Professionnel Paris (Inoa ammonia-free color, Serie Expert, Absolut Repair Molecular) and certified Botoplex peptide smoothing treatments.

Are services customized for both men and women?

Yes! SPARK is a complete luxury unisex destination with dedicated zones for women's hair couture & aesthetic facials, alongside an executive barber lounge for men's fades and beard grooming.

How do bridal bookings and trials work?

We offer comprehensive bridal consultations, skin prep timelines, and trial sessions for hair & makeup. Reach out to our concierge 3 to 6 weeks in advance to schedule your private bridal trial.
