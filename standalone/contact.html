Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting SPARK.

Do I need to book in advance or do you accept walk-ins?

While we welcome walk-ins whenever chair availability allows, we strongly recommend reserving in advance—especially for French Balayage, Keratin treatments, and weekend slots—so our master stylists can dedicate unhurried time to your hair.

What haircare formulations do you use?

We are an authorized partner of L'Oréal Professionnel Paris. All chemical services, color treatments, and hair spas use 100% sealed, authentic imported formulations including Metal Detox, Inoa ammonia-free color, and Botoplex collagen.

Is there dedicated parking available at the salon?

Yes. We offer reserved basement guest parking and complimentary valet service right at our entrance opposite City Centre on the main boulevard.

How does the First-Time Guest Privilege work?

First-time visitors enjoy 20% savings on their initial signature service. Simply mention promo code SPARKFIRST when booking online or present it at reception upon arrival.
