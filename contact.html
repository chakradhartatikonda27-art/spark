Questions & Clarity

Everything You Need to Know

Transparent answers about our hygiene, appointment timings, and formulations.

Do I need to book in advance or do you accept walk-in clients?

We welcome walk-ins based on real-time chair availability. However, to guarantee dedicated 1-on-1 consultation time with our senior colorists or for extensive services like Balayage, Keratin, and Bridal styling, we strongly encourage reserving 24–48 hours ahead.

Which product brands and chemical formulations do you use?

We use 100% genuine, authorized professional products — primarily L'Oréal Professionnel Paris (Inoa ammonia-free color, Metal Detox, Serie Expert), genuine Brazilian Botoplex collagen, and dermatologically tested skincare lines.

Are services customized for both men and women?

Yes, SPARK is a premier unisex salon. We feature dedicated zones for gentlemen’s barbering, hot towel beard sculpting, textured hair artistry, clinical medi-facials, and private bridal makeup suites.

Is there dedicated parking available at the salon?

Yes. We offer reserved basement guest parking and complimentary valet service right at our entrance opposite City Centre on the main boulevard.

How does the First-Time Guest Privilege work?

First-time visitors enjoy 20% savings on their initial signature service. Simply mention promo code SPARKFIRST when booking online or present it at reception upon arrival.
